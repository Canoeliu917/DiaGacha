# DiaGacha·咫牌 v23 brief

Keep v22 mechanics. Three player-facing changes, plus a 706→777 pool expansion.

## Layout
Six result minis stay two rows of three at 390px. Art takes ~64% of each mini; the text rail is a tight 36% column (grade, stars, name). Slot label is full width and now reads like `剧本 · 22%`.

## Display sort
Minis (and posters, which clone the result row) show weight order:

1. 剧本 22%  2. 主角 20%  3. 手段 16%  4. 配角 16%  5. 演法 14%  6. 空间 12%

Scoring, hexBonus, 演后谈, `#m=` share, pack-open and radar stay in source order `[剧目, 风格体系, 导演手法, 主演, 配角, 环境]`. Legacy five-card shares keep the old five-card order, 五芒星 and `这一台没有配角入册`.

## Copy
Every card, including 龙套/班底, now has a unique essay under 看点. Length scales by rarity (C/UC 80–120, R/SR 100–140, SSR 120–170, UR/LR 140–200). Existing `note`/`observe` facts stay verbatim.

## Art
Same paper-cut / geometric language, more figurative at mini size: role props are larger and id-specific; plays/places/styles/methods get a readable emblem. The old `e.slice(500)` thin overwrite no longer wipes specs.

## Pool
71 new cards (`v23-…`), spread 12/12/12/12/12/11 across the six dimensions. Collection total and the complete-set milestone follow `e.length` (777). 100/300/500 unchanged. No 戏曲. Brand 咫牌 only.
